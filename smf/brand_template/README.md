# {{BRAND_NAME}}

Everything about this brand lives in this folder. It stays on your computer.

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `brand-master.json` | The brand's settings: who it is, who it talks to, how it sounds, its colours, its accounts. Onboarding writes this. Ask Claude to change it rather than editing by hand. |
| `Brand/` | The long-form version: the business, the voice, real samples of how you talk, your audience's questions, your offers. Plus `brand-kit/` for logos, photos and fonts. |
| `Inputs/` | Raw material you bring: recordings, research, questions people ask, proof (results, testimonials, screenshots), finished videos. `Inputs/Footage` is the one place for footage and pictures a film may cut from. |
| `Studio/` | Working files the tools keep: topic bank, calls to action, your proof library, files derived from the settings. |
| `Output/` | Finished work: batches of posts, visuals, ready-to-post folders, video. |
| `Strategy/` | Plans: pillars, series, shoot lists. |
| `_state/` | The tools' memory of what was scheduled and posted. Do not edit. |

**The one rule:** the Social Media Factory writes from what is in this folder. It never invents a fact, a number,
a quote or a customer story. The more real material you put in `Brand/` and `Inputs/`, the better it writes.
