# Maple & Main Bakery: who we talk to

## Who they are
Neighbours within a few miles who care about good bread, and home bakers anywhere who want to
understand sourdough well enough to stop following recipes blindly.

## What they are trying to get done
Bake a loaf at home that is open, light and sour in the way they like, every time, without buying equipment.

## What is in their way
Recipes that give times instead of signs. Cold kitchens. Fear of wet dough.

## Questions they really ask
- Why is my sourdough dense?
- How do I know when the dough has proofed enough?
- Can I bake good bread in a normal oven?
- What does "naturally leavened" actually mean?
- How do I keep a starter alive when I travel?
- Why did my loaf spread flat instead of rising?

## Objections we hear
- "I do not have time for bread that takes two days." (Most of that time you are asleep.)
- "A class is expensive for one loaf." (You leave with the method, not the loaf.)
