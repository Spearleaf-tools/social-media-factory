# Maple & Main Bakery: real samples of how we talk

(Invented for the example brand. In your own brand this file holds things you really wrote or said.)

---

**A note Ava wrote for the class handout**

Your dough is not late. It is cold. At 18 degrees a loaf that takes five hours in my bakery takes
eight in your kitchen. Nothing is wrong. Watch the dough, not the clock. When it has grown by half
and wobbles like a set custard when you shake the bowl, it is ready. Not before.

---

**Ava, answering a customer who said her loaf was a brick**

I made bricks for my first four months. Every one had the same cause: I shaped too early because
the recipe said "four hours". The recipe did not know my kitchen. Give it the time it asks for.
Time is an ingredient, and it is the only free one.

---

**The shop, on a Friday**

Rye is out at seven. There are twelve. When they are gone they are gone, and we will not pretend
otherwise. Subscribers, yours is kept back with your name on it.

---

**Ava, on equipment**

You do not need a special oven. You need a pot with a lid. The lid traps the steam your dough makes,
and steam is what lets the crust stretch before it sets. A ten pound casserole does what a steam oven does.

---

**The shop, announcing a class**

Saturday class, two until six. Eight places. You will mix, fold, shape and bake one loaf, and you
will leave with a jar of our starter. Wet hands, light touch. That is most of it.
