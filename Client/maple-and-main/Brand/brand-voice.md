# Maple & Main Bakery: how we sound

## In a sentence or two
Warm, plain and specific. A baker talking across the counter: short sentences, real quantities,
no romance about flour.

## We do
- Give the number: grams, hours, degrees.
- Explain the why before the how.
- Admit what went wrong in our own bakes.

## We do not
- Call anything artisanal.
- Pretend good bread is hard.
- Make anyone feel bad about supermarket bread.

## Words and phrases that are really ours
- "Time is an ingredient."
- "Watch the dough, not the clock."
- "Wet hands, light touch."

## Who is speaking
**Ava** says "I". She talks from her own bench: what she tried, what failed, what she does now.
**Maple & Main** says "you" and "we". It talks to the neighbour: what is fresh, what is on, how to join in.
