# Maple & Main Bakery: what we talk about, and what we offer

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Why it matters to the audience | Example topics |
|---|---|---|
| How bread actually works | People who understand the why stop needing recipes | Why cold dough is slow dough; what steam does to a crust |
| Fix my loaf | Everyone has a failed loaf and wants one cause and one fix | Dense crumb; flat loaf; gummy middle; pale crust |
| Behind the counter | Trust comes from seeing the real day, including the mistakes | The 4am mix; the batch we threw away; why rye sells out |
| Bake with us | The class is how neighbours become bakers | What you leave with; who it suits; what people get wrong in week one |
