# Maple & Main Bakery: the business

(An invented business. See the README in this folder.)

## What we do, and for whom
We bake naturally leavened bread every morning for the people of Alder Falls, and on Saturdays we
teach eight home bakers at a time how to make it themselves.

## Where we work
One shop on the corner of Maple Street and Main Street. Classes happen in the bakery after closing.

## What makes us different
- Every loaf ferments for at least eighteen hours. We do not use commercial yeast in the sourdough.
- The bakers teach the classes. There is no separate "school".
- We publish our methods. Nothing about our bread is a secret.

## What we sell
| Offer | Who it is for | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday sourdough class | Home bakers who want to understand, not just follow | Book online, arrive at two, leave at six with a loaf and a starter |
| Weekly bread subscription | Neighbours who want their loaf kept back | Reserve online, collect Friday or Saturday |

## Facts we are happy to state in public
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Our sourdough ferments for at least 18 hours | Our own production sheet |
| A class takes 8 people and lasts 4 hours | Our class booking page |
| We opened in 2019 | Our shop lease |
| Starter hydration is 100 percent (equal flour and water by weight) | Our own method sheet |

## Things we never say
We never name the cafes and shops we supply. We never call our bread gluten-free.
